🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to ReadyCheck, an offline-first Progressive Web App designed for managing checklists seamlessly on land or at sea.

  • View Checklists: Tap or click any checklist card on the main screen to open and interactively check off items.
  • Search: Use the top search bar to quickly filter your checklist library by title.

📝 Creating & Editing Checklists

Tailor your checklists to your exact requirements:

  • + New Checklist: Tap to create a brand new checklist from scratch.
  • Edit Mode: Add, edit, or remove sub-lists, update item titles and descriptions, and reorder items.
  • Copy Checklist: Duplicate existing checklists to make quick variations.

📥 Import & Export

Easily back up or transfer your checklist data:

  • Import Checklist: Tap 📥 Import to load a .json checklist from your local storage or from a web url.
  • Bluetooth & Web Share: Share checklists wirelessly with nearby devices or save them to your local device storage.

📚 Restoring Defaults

Reset your checklist library back to factory preloaded templates:

  • Tap the Defaults button on the main toolbar to restore preloaded vessel and equipment checklists.
